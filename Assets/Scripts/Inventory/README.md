INPUTS:

Press tab to turn on/off the main inventory
Press 1-6 outside of the inventory to assign that hotbar's item to the player's equipped slot.
Press 1-6 while hovering over an item in the inventory to assign that item to a hotslot.
Left click an item in the inventory to start moving it. Then left click the TOP LEFT of the group of slots you want to move it to. (If you miss you gotta start this process again)
The ability to open a journal entry has been assigned to right click in this patch.
Press 'e' to add an axe to the inventory (10 damage, max stack x1)
Press 'r' to add a sword to the inventory (20 damage, max stack x2)
Press 't' to add an apple to the inventory (not a weapon, max stack x5)

You cannot currently rotate items within the inventory, drop items, or split stacks.
The UI of the inventory awaits improvement.

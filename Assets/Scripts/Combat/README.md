## Combat prototype

Open Assets/Scenes/CombatTest.unity and press Play.

Left click to swing the axe, it is the only weapon for now you must equip it first otherwise your clicks will do nothing. Attacks aim through the crosshair in the centre of the screen, rather than the mouse pointer.
The camera is stationary in this test scene, currently waiting for movement and inventory to be made a bit more.

The axe deals 10 damage it has a range of 2.5 units and a cooldown of
0.5 seconds. The dummy has 100 health and disappears after 10 hits.
Stop and restart Play mode to reset it.
